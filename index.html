Meet Your Trainer

AFTAB ANSARI

IT Trainer — Linux · AWS · DevOps

Aftab teaches Linux administration, cloud infrastructure and DevOps engineering with a single focus: getting hands dirty on real systems, not just reading slides. Every topic is grounded in the kind of tasks you'll actually face — configuring servers, designing cloud architecture, building deployment pipelines and troubleshooting when things break.

The training is built around practical labs, real-world scenarios and project-based learning, so what you learn in a session is something you can immediately do yourself — and explain confidently in an interview.
